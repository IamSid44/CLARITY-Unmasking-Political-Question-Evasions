\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A classifier on this task is limited by knowledge, not structure, and ranks better than it decides. A 27B adjudicator consulted only where it is unsure lifts dev \Stwo{} to 0.650 with fewer LLM calls and far fewer tokens than the winning system, and a distilled student matches the ten-model classifier at a quarter of its GPU time.
As a frozen instrument, it shows that evasion is a relation between question and answer that relevance does not capture, that in the President's role LLMs answer directly what the Presidents answered directly (exploratory), and that the cascade's score stays within its own noise when its first stage runs in FP8.
